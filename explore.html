ABOUT THIS PROJECT

SPACE: The Forgotten Legacy

Exploring the stories humanity left behind

Landing is only the beginning of a mission. Our entry for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, Space Forgotten, follows what happens after: the hardware left on the Moon, on Mars and beyond, and the stories and science they still carry.
